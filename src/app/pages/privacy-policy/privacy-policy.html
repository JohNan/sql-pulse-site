Privacy Policy for SQL Pulse - Database Studio

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled in SQL Pulse - Database Studio (application ID com.johnan.mysqlclient, the "App"), developed and published by Nanzen.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. SQL Pulse is designed as an on-device developer utility with zero reliance on external intermediary servers.

Information Collection and Storage

The App does not directly collect, store, transmit, or harvest any personally identifiable information (PII). We do not require you to create an account, register an email address, or log into any Nanzen cloud service to use the App.

On-Device Credential Security

All database connection profiles, hostnames, port numbers, usernames, database passwords, and SSH private keys or passphrases are stored strictly on your local device. Credentials are encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption, with cryptographic keys securely anchored in the hardware-backed Android Keystore (Secure Element / StrongBox Keymaster). Your credentials never leave your device and are never transmitted to Nanzen or any third party.

Direct Network Architecture

When executing queries or inspecting schemas, the App establishes direct, encrypted socket connections from your Android device to your configured MySQL/MariaDB database server or SSH bastion jump-host. There are no intermediate proxy servers, cloud relays, or Nanzen infrastructure processing or observing your SQL queries, schema metadata, or result sets.

Third-Party Services

To facilitate software licensing, in-app billing, stability diagnostics, and operational performance, the App integrates select third-party SDKs provided by Google. These third-party services may collect pseudonymized device identifiers and telemetry in accordance with their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services & Google Play Billing: Used to manage app licensing, In-App Updates, and Google Play Pro tier transactions.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Used to collect crash diagnostics, stack traces, and device hardware specifications to fix software defects.
  • Firebase Analytics: Used to collect anonymized usage events and engagement metrics to improve user interface workflows.

These services may record technical metadata such as your device IP address, operating system version, device model, and timestamped error logs.

Cookies and Local Storage

The mobile application itself does not use browser HTTP cookies. On-device settings and preferences are stored locally using Android Jetpack DataStore and encrypted Room SQLite databases. Our marketing website may use minimal functional local storage for theme selection (Light/Dark mode).

Security

We value your trust in choosing SQL Pulse for managing critical infrastructure. We implement industry-standard security safeguards, including hardware-backed Android Keystore cryptography, biometric authentication locks, strict SSH host-key verification (TOFU), and destructive SQL query interception. However, please remember that database security also depends on keeping your device secure and configuring appropriate database user privileges.

Links to Other Sites

The App or marketing site may contain links to third-party websites or services (e.g., external documentation or Google Play). If you click on a third-party link, you will be directed to that external site. We have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content or privacy practices of external sites.

Children's Privacy

SQL Pulse is a professional database management utility intended for developers and system administrators. It is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new software capabilities, regulatory requirements, or Google Play policy updates. Any revisions will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
